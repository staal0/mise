## Ingredients

- 200 g flour
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1 pinch salt
- 2 eggs
- 3 dl milk
- 1½ tbsp melted butter, plus more for frying

## Instructions

1. Whisk flour, baking powder and salt in a bowl.
2. Whisk in eggs, milk and melted butter until just combined. A few lumps are fine.
3. Let the batter rest for 5 minutes.
4. Fry in butter on medium heat, about 1 minute per side, until golden.

## Notes

Add blueberries to the pan right after pouring the batter.
