## Ingredients

### Meat sauce

- 500 g minced beef
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 can (400 g) chopped tomatoes
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tsp dried oregano

### Béchamel

- 50 g butter
- 50 g flour
- 7,5 dl milk
- ¼ tsp nutmeg

### Assembly

- 12 lasagne sheets
- 100 g grated cheese

## Instructions

### Meat sauce

1. Brown the beef with onion and garlic.
2. Add tomatoes, tomato paste and oregano. Simmer for 20 minutes.

### Béchamel

1. Melt the butter, whisk in the flour and cook for 1 minute.
2. Whisk in the milk a little at a time. Simmer until thick, then season with nutmeg, salt and pepper.

### Assembly

1. Layer sauce, sheets and béchamel in a dish, three times over.
2. Top with cheese and bake at 200 °C for 45 minutes.
